# Ingredients - Makes 1 rack (about 3 lbs.), split into two packets

## Ribs
* 1 rack baby back ribs (about 3 lbs.), cut into two large pieces

## Dry Rub
* 7 tsp. brown sugar
* 1 tsp. chili powder
* 1 tsp. salt
* 1 tsp. garlic powder

## Wet Mix
* 7 tbsp. light soy sauce
* 3 tbsp. honey or maple syrup
* 2 tbsp. Shaoxing cooking wine
* 7-8 garlic cloves, minced
* 1 tbsp. vinegar
* 3 tbsp. water

# Recipe

## Prep
* Mix the dry rub ingredients until even, then divide in half. Rub one half evenly over each piece of ribs.
* Wrap each piece separately in foil and refrigerate at least 2-3 hours.

## Bake
* Mix the minced garlic with the rest of the wet ingredients, then divide in half. Pour one half over each piece of ribs.
* Wrap each piece tightly in enough foil to seal it completely. Do not puncture the foil. The sealed packet steams and bakes at the same time, so a leak-free seal is the most important step.
* Preheat oven to 375°F.
* Bake both packets together for 1 hour 15 minutes, until the meat pulls away from the bone.
