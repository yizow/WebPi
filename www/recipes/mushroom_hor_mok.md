# Ingredients - Makes 16–18 four-ounce foil ramekins, filled ¾ full

## Mushrooms
* 1.5 lbs. cremini mushrooms, thinly sliced
* 1.5 tbsp. neutral oil
* ¾ tsp. kosher salt

## Custard
* 3 cans (14 oz. each) Aroy-D coconut milk, chilled at least 2 hours
  * Skim ¾ cup of the thick solid cream off the top for the cap, and measure 3 ¾ cups of the rest for the custard; discard any watery excess
* 4 tbsp. Mae Ploy (or MaeSri) red curry paste, plus a tiny dab for garnish
* 1 tbsp. sweet basil chili paste (optional)
* 4 whole makrut lime leaves
* 6 large eggs + 3 egg yolks, room temperature (or 7 whole eggs)
* 3 tbsp. fish sauce
* 1 tbsp. low-sodium soy sauce (use 2 tbsp. if using a mild supermarket curry paste)
* 3 tbsp. packed light brown sugar

## Cream Cap
* ¾ cup skimmed coconut cream
* ¼ tsp. kosher salt

## Serving
* Bin Bin rice crackers (or Lundberg thin rice cakes)
* Crispy fried shallots

# Recipe

## Prep
* Preheat oven to 325°F (163°C). Put a kettle of water on to boil.
* Line 16–18 foil ramekins inside one or two deep roasting pans.
* Open the chilled coconut milk cans without shaking. Spoon ¾ cup of the thick solid cream from the top into a small bowl, stir in the ¼ tsp. salt, and set aside. Pour the rest of the coconut milk into a measuring cup; you need 3 ¾ cups.

## Cook the Mushrooms
* Heat 1.5 tbsp. oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add half the mushrooms and a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until all the liquid evaporates and the edges brown deeply, 5–6 minutes.
* Transfer to a bowl and repeat with the remaining oil, mushrooms, and salt.
* Divide the cooked mushrooms evenly among the foil cups.

## Warm the Curry Base
* Turn the skillet down to medium-low. Add the curry paste (and sweet basil paste, if using) and stir for 1 minute until fragrant.
* Pour in the 3 ¾ cups coconut milk and add the lime leaves. Whisk until smooth and warm to the touch — do not boil.
* Take off the heat.

## Make the Custard
* In a large bowl or pitcher, whisk the eggs, yolks, fish sauce, soy sauce, and brown sugar until smooth.
* Slowly whisk in the warm coconut-curry mixture.
* Optional: give it 3–4 quick pulses with an immersion blender to smooth out any paste lumps.
* Pour through a fine-mesh strainer to catch the lime leaves and any lumps.

## Assemble
* Divide the custard evenly over the mushrooms, filling each cup about ¾ full.
* Spoon 1 tsp. of the reserved thick coconut cream onto the center of each cup, and swirl a pinpoint of raw curry paste into the white cream with a toothpick.

## Bake
* Put the roasting pan in the oven and carefully pour hot water into the pan until it comes halfway up the sides of the cups.
* Lay a sheet of foil loosely over the top and bake for 18–22 minutes.
* When it's done: the edges are set and matte, the center still wobbles slightly like Jell-O (around 170°F), and the surface is flat. If it starts puffing up like a dome or pulling away from the edges, pull it immediately.

## Cool and Store
* Turn off the oven, crack the door open, and let the cups sit in the hot water bath for 10 minutes.
* Move them to the counter to cool completely, then snap on the lids and refrigerate up to 24 hours.

## Serving
* Pull the cups out 45–60 minutes before guests arrive (leave the lids on while warming so they don't dry out).
* Serve at room temperature with rice crackers and a bowl of crispy fried shallots on the side.
* Put out half the batch first and swap in the rest halfway through the party.
